BOB, Budget on Board: phone app package

index.html            the app
manifest.webmanifest  name, icon and colours for the home screen
sw.js                 keeps the app usable offline
icons/                app icons
api/bob.js            Bob's AI (server function, needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY on Vercel)
vercel.json           gives Bob up to 60 seconds per answer

Follow the guide "BOB on Your Phone" step by step.
